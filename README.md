# Arkiv × Devfolio: Builder Challenge, India Edition

**Global Tour Stop · Road to Devcon Mumbai · 9–18 October 2026 · online**

> [!NOTE]
> **Draft.** These rules are being finalised and will be frozen when the challenge opens on 9 October. Until then, details may change.

Build on [Arkiv](https://arkiv.network), the Web3 database, around one theme: **CROPS**, the Ethereum Foundation's mandate that Ethereum stay **c**ensorship resistant, **o**pen source, **p**rivate and **s**ecure.

Ten days, fully online, open to builders everywhere. Five winners get a Devcon 8 ticket, USDC, and a slot to demo at the Arkiv booth in Mumbai.

## How it is judged

Every project picks **one CROPS pillar**. All projects are scored on the same four criteria, 25 points each:

| Criterion | The question |
|---|---|
| **Why this pillar?** | Who is harmed today if this data is censored, shut down, leaked or forged? |
| **Why Arkiv?** | What would break on Postgres, IPFS or your own API, and what did you keep off Arkiv on purpose? |
| **How you use Arkiv** | How and why you use each Arkiv feature. Not how many you use. |
| **Friction report** | Specific, reproducible feedback on building with Arkiv |

Before scoring, every project must pass the gates: it works, it can be reproduced, and it ships the required evidence. Full rubric: [docs/scoring-rubric.md](docs/scoring-rubric.md). What to submit: [docs/submission-checklist.md](docs/submission-checklist.md).
